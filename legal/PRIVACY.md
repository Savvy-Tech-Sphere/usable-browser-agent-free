# Privacy Policy

**Product:** Usable Browser Agent
**Publisher:** Savvy Tech Sphere, LLC
**Seller of record:** Lemon Squeezy, LLC
**Last updated:** 2026-09-02

> Short version: The Software runs entirely on your machine. It collects no usage data, contains no analytics or telemetry, and sends nothing back to us. The only personal data involved in a purchase is handled by our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy, so that you can be charged and invoiced.

## The Software collects no data

Usable Browser Agent runs locally on your computer. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no "phone home." It does not transmit your browsing activity, the pages it acts on, the credentials in your local vault, your workflow memory, or any other usage information to Savvy Tech Sphere or to any third party.

Everything the Software stores stays on your machine:

- **Credential vault** (`~/.config/usable-browser-agent/secrets.json`): the logins you choose to store, kept locally with restrictive file permissions. Credentials are passed only to your own browser over a local loopback connection and are never sent to the AI model or to any server.
- **Workflow memory** (`~/.local/state/usable-browser-agent/workflow-memory.jsonl`): reusable, non-secret notes about how a site's flow works, stored locally.

You can delete either file at any time to remove that data.

## Your AI agent and the sites you use

The Software is operated by an AI agent (for example Claude Code or Codex) that you run, and it acts on websites you choose. Your separate agreements with your AI provider and with those websites govern how they handle data. The page content the agent reads is processed by your AI provider as part of running your agent; that flow is between you and your provider. The Software itself adds no additional collection or transmission.

When you buy a license, the purchase is processed by Lemon Squeezy, LLC, our seller of record. Lemon Squeezy collects the information needed to take payment and issue an invoice, which may include your name, email address, billing location, and payment details. Savvy Tech Sphere receives limited order information (such as your email and the fact of purchase) to deliver your license key and download, and to handle refunds.

Lemon Squeezy's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy, available at <https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy>.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
